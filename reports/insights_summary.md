# Sugar Cosmetics – Instagram Insights & Strategic Recommendations

#### 1. Key takeaways

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Overall performance** | 50,000 posts (19 Apr 2024 – 19 Apr 2025) generated **1.28B impressions**, **987M reach**, **147.7M likes** and **18.5M comments**. Average engagement rate is **18.54%** of reach. |
| **Posting consistency** | ~**4,174 posts per full month** (~137 per day). Volume is very stable month to month; Apr 2024 and Apr 2025 are partial months. |
| **Engagement mix** | Likes are **80.7%** of all engagement. Comments are 10.1%, saves 4.8% and shares 4.4%. Saves and shares are the "high-intent" actions the algorithm rewards most, and they are the weakest part of the mix. |
| **Funnel** | Impressions → Reach 77.4% → Likes 15.0% of reach → Comments 1.87% → Shares 0.82% → Saves 0.90%. The biggest drop is between *liking* and any deeper action. |
| **Follower growth** | 3.0% of reached users visit the profile, and **29.9% of profile visitors follow**. The profile converts well; the bottleneck is getting people to visit it. Monthly follower gain is steady (~178 follows per post), with no spikes and no stagnation. |
| **Post format** | Reel (18.61%) and Story (18.60%) lead, ahead of Video (18.51%) and Image (18.43%). The gap is only 0.18 pp (ANOVA p = 0.15, not significant). |
| **Best days** | Wednesday (18.63%), Saturday (18.60%) and Friday (18.57%) lead. Sunday is weakest (18.42%). Weekday vs weekend is almost identical (18.55% vs 18.51%). |
| **Hashtags** | #VeganBeauty (18.61%) and #BoldLooks (18.58%) are the strongest tags; #MakeupGoals (18.51%) is the weakest. Posts with **2 hashtags** do best (18.63%), and 1-hashtag posts do worst (18.44%), the closest result to significance (p = 0.054). |
| **Campaigns** | Influencer Collab (18.60%) and Tutorial (18.58%) lead; Festive Offer is lowest (18.44%). The best format × campaign combination is **Story + Tutorial (18.80%)**. Tutorials make up the largest share of top-quartile posts (21.1%). |
| **Paid vs organic** | Paid posts (18.53% ER, 19.6K avg reach) do **not** clearly outperform Organic posts (18.44% ER, 20.0K avg reach). Paid spend is not buying extra reach per post. |
| **Hidden gems** | Some of the highest-ER posts had small reach. For example, P48036 (Video, Product Launch) reached only 7.2K people but hit 34.0% ER. Those audiences engaged strongly, so these posts are good candidates to boost or repurpose. |

> **Important context:** performance is remarkably uniform across every category (format, day, hashtag, campaign, month). All differences are under 0.4 percentage points and most are not statistically significant. This tells us that *timing, format and hashtags are not the levers*. Content quality and creative are. The recommendations below reflect that.

#### 2. Recommended posting strategy

- **Timing:** Keep the steady 7-day schedule, since engagement is consistent across days. Put flagship launches and key campaigns on **Wednesday, Friday or Saturday**, and keep Sunday for lighter content.
- **Frequency:** Volume is already very high (~137 posts/day) and consistent. Hold frequency flat and **shift effort from volume to quality**. More posts are not lifting engagement rate.
- **Content types:** Lead with **Reels and Stories**, especially **Story + Tutorial** and **Reel + Influencer Collab** combinations. Reduce plain Image posts for offers (Image + Festive Offer is among the weakest combos).
- **Hashtags:** Use **2–3 focused hashtags** per post and avoid single-tag posts. Prioritise **#VeganBeauty** and **#BoldLooks**, and keep **#SugarCosmetics** as the brand tag.

#### 3. Suggestions to improve engagement & follower growth

1. **Design for saves and shares:** swatch charts, "shade finder" carousels, step-by-step tutorials and "save this look" prompts. These lift the 0.9% save rate and 0.8% share rate.
2. **Drive profile visits:** only 3% of reach visits the profile, but 30% of visitors follow. Use clear CTAs ("full shade range on our profile"), pinned highlights and collab tags that send people to the profile.
3. **Spark comments:** ask questions, run "this or that" polls in Stories, and reply to comments in the first hour.
4. **Re-evaluate paid boosts:** paid posts don't beat organic on ER or reach. Shift budget toward boosting proven **hidden-gem** posts (high ER, low reach) instead of boosting new posts blindly.
5. **Repurpose winners:** turn top-ER tutorials into Reels, Stories and carousels.

#### 4. Gaps identified & how to address them

| Gap | Impact | How to address |
|---|---|---|
| No **caption** text | Can't analyse keywords, tone or CTA use | Export captions from Meta Business Suite and add text analysis |
| No **posting time** (hour) | Can't recommend best hour of day | Capture timestamp with hour; analyse hour × weekday |
| No **follower count** over time | Growth is measured only via follows from posts | Track daily follower count and unfollows |
| No **ad spend** | Can't measure paid ROI | Add spend per boosted post → cost per engagement / per follow |
| Very uniform data | Category differences are tiny | Run A/B tests (same creative, different format/time) to confirm real effects |
| Carousel not a separate type | Can't compare carousel vs single image | Tag carousel posts separately |

#### 5. Proposed content & campaign ideas

- **"Tutorial Tuesdays / Wednesdays" Story series:** 60-second looks using one hero product. This uses the best combo, Story + Tutorial.
- **#BoldLooks creator collab Reels:** monthly influencer collab challenge with a branded hashtag, user-generated content reposted to Stories.
- **#VeganBeauty education carousels:** ingredient explainers and cruelty-free proof points, designed to be saved.
- **Shade-match giveaway:** comment your skin tone and get a shade recommendation, with a giveaway entry for following plus sharing. This drives comments, shares and follows together.
- **Festive offer refresh:** move festive offers from static images to Reels and Stories with a tutorial angle ("3 party looks under ₹999").
